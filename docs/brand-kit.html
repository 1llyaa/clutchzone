Brand kit for generated artwork

Everything needed to produce on-brand Instagram posts, stories, posters and price cards for Clutch Zone, an esport gaming club in České Budějovice.

This page is the single source of truth. Paste it, or its URL, into a design tool as project context. Every value below is taken from the live site's globals.css, so artwork and web stay in step.

Color

Palette

Near-black ground, one orange accent, a narrow grey ramp. There is no second accent hue. Semantic colors are for status only and never decorate.

Core

Orange#E84A1A The single accent. CTAs, active state, hairline rules, key numbers.
Orange Dark#B83A12 Hover and pressed only. Never a fill on its own.
Orange Glow#FF6B35 Glow and focus ring. Not for text or large fills.
Black#0A0A0A Primary ground. The default background of every layout.
Black Mid#111111 Cards and panels sitting on the ground.
Black Light#1A1A1A Nested surfaces, inputs, code blocks.

Line and text

Gray Dark#2A2A2A Borders and dividers.
Gray Mid#555555 Muted text. Too dark for body copy on black.
Gray Light#888888 Secondary and supporting text.
White Soft#E8E8E8 Body copy. Softer than pure white, easier on a dark ground.
White#FFFFFF Headlines and display type only.
Hairlinergba(255,255,255,.06) Section rules and the grid overlay.

Status

Success#22C55EConfirmed, paid, available.
Warning#EAB308Pending, expiring.
Danger#EF4444Cancelled, failed, full.

Rules

Do
  • Keep orange rare. Two or three accents per layout is the ceiling.
  • Layer surfaces in order: black, then black-mid, then black-light.
  • Use white for display type, white-soft for reading copy.
  • Hold text contrast at WCAG AA or better.
Don't
  • No second accent hue. No purple, teal, or blue anywhere.
  • No orange text on an orange fill, no white on white.
  • No gradients except the one orange radial glow, at 0.15 opacity or less.
  • No color outside this palette, including in photography treatments.

Fitting headlines

Archivo at width 125 and weight 700 measures roughly 0.86 em per uppercase character. That is close to double a condensed face, so a headline that fits in a comp using Oswald or Bebas will overflow here.

Estimate the width of a line before committing to a size: characters × 0.86 × font-size. A 12 character word at 100px needs about 1030px, which will not fit inside a 1080px canvas once margins are counted. Either shorten the line or drop the size.

Cyrillic

Archivo's Latin coverage is what the brand is built on. For Ukrainian, the site swaps display and label to Oswald and body to Inter. Match that in artwork rather than letting Archivo fall back silently.

Graphic language

Signature devices

Four devices carry the brand. Use one or two per layout. Using all four at once reads as noise.

1. Grid overlay

A 48px technical grid behind hero and poster backgrounds, masked so it fades out. This is the most recognisable element of the brand.

background-image:
  linear-gradient(rgba(255,255,255,0.045) 1px, transparent 1px),
  linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.045) 1px, transparent 1px);
background-size: 48px 48px;
mask-image: radial-gradient(ellipse 90% 80% at 70% 35%, #000 30%, transparent 85%);

2. Orange radial glow

One warm bloom, usually top right, behind the subject. Never more than one, never above 0.15 opacity.

background: radial-gradient(circle, rgba(232,74,26,0.15), transparent 62%);
width: 760px; height: 760px;

3. Corner brackets

Thin orange brackets on two opposing corners to frame a price, a score, or a featured item. Two corners, never four.

285 Kč

4. Accent underline

A 2px orange rule under one heading per layout, or along the top edge of a single panel. It marks the one thing that matters most.

Logo

The mark

A filled target: alternating cream and near-black concentric rings with a solid orange core, on a near-black rounded tile. The rings are thick filled bands, not thin strokes. Paste this SVG rather than redrawing it.

  <rect x="1" y="1" width="98" height="98" rx="13" fill="none"
        stroke="rgba(255,255,255,0.08)" stroke-width="0.6"/>

  <circle cx="50" cy="50" r="9"  fill="#E84A1A"/>
</svg>
Do
  • Pair the mark with the wordmark set in display type, tracked 2px.
  • Add the subtitle "ESPORT CLUB · ČB" in label style, orange.
  • Keep clear space of at least half the tile width on every side.
  • Minimum size 32px. Below that the inner rings close up.
Don't
  • Never recolor the rings or the core.
  • Never place the mark on an orange or light background.
  • Never stretch, rotate, or add effects to it.
  • Never rebuild it as thin concentric outlines.
Voice

Writing the copy

RuleDetail
LanguageCzech for local artwork. Informal "ty", the way players speak to each other.
HeadlinesAlways uppercase, always display type. Short. Two or three words per line.
RegisterDirect and confident. No filler, no hype adjectives, no exclamation marks in running text.
Calls to actionActive verbs: REZERVOVAT, HRÁT TEĎ, ZOBRAZIT CENÍK, PŘIHLÁSIT SE. One intent per label.
PricesAmount then "Kč", no decimals unless genuinely needed. Example: 285 Kč.
Times24 hour clock. Ranges use a hyphen: 14:00-17:00.
DashesUse a plain hyphen. No em dash and no en dash anywhere in visible copy.
MultiplicationWrite a plain letter x, not the × sign. It reads better in tracked uppercase.
EyebrowsSparingly. If every block has a small label above it, they stop meaning anything.
